# Retail Sales Analysis & Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data from the Sample Superstore dataset to identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, and generate business insights. The analysis focuses on understanding product category performance, regional profitability, discount impacts, and top-performing products.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into actionable insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Questions

This project aims to answer the following questions:

1. What are the total sales and total profit?
2. How many orders and customers are there, and what is the average sales per order?
3. What are the monthly sales trends?
4. Which product category generates the highest profit?
5. Which regions have high sales but low profit?
6. What is the relationship between discounts and profit?
7. What is the relationship between total sales and profit across product categories?
8. What are the top 5 products by sales, and which categories do they belong to?

## Dataset

- **Dataset:** Sample Superstore
- **Description:** Retail transaction data containing order details, customer information, product categories, sales, quantity, discounts, and profit.
- **Source:** [Kaggle – Sample Superstore Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)

- **Pandas:** Data manipulation and analysis
- **SQL (MySQL):** Data querying, aggregation, and business analysis
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- **Excel / Google Sheets:** Spreadsheet-based data management, where applicable

## Analysis Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Load and inspect the dataset to understand its structure and data quality.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Check data types, missing values, duplicates, and numeric formatting to ensure data consistency.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Analyze sales, profit, orders, customers, and product performance using Python and SQL.
4. **Business Analysis:** Investigate monthly sales trends, regional profitability, discount-profit relationships, and category performance.
5. **Data Visualization:** Present key findings through charts and dashboards in Power BI.
6. **Business Insights:** Identify areas for further investigation and potential improvements in profitability.

## Key Findings

- **Category Performance:** Technology generated the highest total profit, followed by Office Supplies and Furniture.
- **Monthly Sales Trends:** Sales fluctuated throughout 2014–2017, with notable peaks in March, September, and November.
- **Regional Profitability:** Texas and Pennsylvania recorded high sales but negative profit.
- **Discount and Profit:** Higher discount levels were associated with more loss-making transactions.
- **Sales vs. Profit:** Higher sales did not always translate into higher profit across product categories.
- **Top Products:** The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier led in both sales and profit, while some high-selling products generated low or negative profit.

## Business Recommendations

- Review discount policies, especially at higher discount levels, to understand their impact on profitability.
- Investigate loss-making regions by examining product categories, discounts, and transaction-level profit.
- Evaluate Furniture's product-level margins and sales performance to identify opportunities to improve profitability.
- Monitor sales and profit together rather than using sales volume as the only performance indicator.

These recommendations identify areas for further investigation; additional analysis is required to establish the specific causes of low profitability.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Python, SQL, and Power BI to analyze retail performance and communicate business insights. By examining sales trends, category performance, regional profitability, and discount patterns, the analysis highlights why profitability should be evaluated alongside sales when making business decisions.
